ccdcam

analog ghosts on a glass plate

an android camcorder app with a hand-tuned ccd shader written in glsl. recreates the look of early consumer camcorders: vertical highlight smear, chroma noise, scanlines, warm color grade.

free alternative to dazz cam, fully open source. live preview, recording, adjustable shader parameters, no telemetry.
